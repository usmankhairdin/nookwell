Can I browse both sales and rentals?

Yes. Use Buy or Rent above the sample listings, then narrow the results by neighborhood. Choose All homes and Everywhere to see the whole shortlist again.

How do I ask about a home?

Open a home's details and choose Ask about this home. Its name will be added to your enquiry. This static demo prepares an email draft; it doesn't send a message or book a viewing.

Are these real homes for sale?

No. Nookwell is a fictional real-estate studio, and the listings, prices, addresses and availability are sample content. The photography illustrates the template; it does not represent a real property offer.

Can I make this website my own?

Absolutely. This is a free HTML, CSS and JavaScript template. Replace the homes, text, brand and contact destination, keeping the included font, library and photo license notices. Setup and domain-upload instructions are in README.txt.
